Our Story

Words and Wheels \u2014 About

A creative publishing and entertainment hub — books, games, music, comics, and more.

Words and Wheels was born from a simple belief: great stories should be accessible to everyone, anywhere, at any moment. What started as a digital bookstore has grown into a creative publishing and entertainment hub — books, arcade games, original music, comic strips, phrasebooks, and a blog, all created by Bob Suter.

Our catalog spans fiction, thriller, memoir, and creative nonfiction. Whether it's the gripping Salvager series by Jack Baptiste, the warm heart of Happiness Finds Big Man, or the nostalgic world of Valley Fair, every book we carry is chosen because it earns its place on your reading list. Beyond the books, Bob's Arcade offers free games inspired by the characters, Bob's Jukebox features 23 original songs, and the Salvager Comics section expands Zeke Carter's world panel by panel.

All books are published by R. E. Suter and available exclusively as instant digital downloads. No shipping. No waiting. Just great reading — plus free games, music, and comics to explore.

Meet the Author

Words and Wheels \u2014 Robert Suter

Robert Suter is a wheelchair user, author, publisher, songwriter, and creator living in Toms River, New Jersey. He writes with determination and imagination under the pen names Jack Baptiste and R. E. Suter, proving that stories don't retire.

R. E. Suter is the publisher and creative force behind Words and Wheels — a creative publishing and entertainment hub built on the belief that great stories should reach anyone, anywhere, instantly.

From the gripping thriller world of The Salvager series to the warmth of Happiness Finds Big Man and the nostalgic charm of Just Another Typical Day in Valley Fair, every book in the catalog reflects a commitment to storytelling that earns its place in your hands.

All books are available as instant digital downloads — no shipping, no waiting. Just great reading, delivered to your device the moment you're ready.
