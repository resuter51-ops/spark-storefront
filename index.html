Meet the Author

Robert Suter

Robert Suter, born in Newark, New Jersey, now resides at Hampton Ridge Healthcare and Rehabilitation in Toms River, New Jersey. He is confined to a wheelchair. At 74, he continues to write with determination and imagination under the pen names Jack Baptiste and R. E. Suter, proving that stories don't retire.

R. E. Suter is the publisher and creative force behind Words and Wheels — a digital bookstore built on the belief that great stories should reach anyone, anywhere, instantly.

From the gripping thriller world of The Salvager series to the warmth of Happiness Finds Big Man and the nostalgic charm of Just Another Typical Day in Valley Fair, every book in the catalog reflects a commitment to storytelling that earns its place in your hands.

All books are available as instant digital downloads — no shipping, no waiting. Just great reading, delivered to your device the moment you're ready.
